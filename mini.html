        The link web behind the ranking

        Every arrow is a link from one page to another, and every link is a vote. Hover or tap a page to see who votes for it.

        PageRank scores

        Round 0
          1. 1Everyone starts equalAll six pages begin with the same score, 1 divided by 6.
          2. 2Pages share their scoreA page splits its score between the pages it links to. A link from a strong page is worth more.
          3. 3Repeat until stableAfter a few dozen rounds the scores stop moving. That final number is the PageRank.

          Formula: PR(page) = 0.15 / 6 + 0.85 × sum of (PR(linking page) ÷ its number of outgoing links). The 0.85 is the damping factor: a visitor follows a link 85% of the time and jumps to a random page 15% of the time. PageRank is worked out once for the whole web and is the same for every search. Your query only decides which pages are allowed into the list.
